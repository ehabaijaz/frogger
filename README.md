## Features 🚕
<p> This is a frogger-inspired game where the player must reach home as fast as possible and avoid getting hit by the cars. </p>
<p> The average time to reach home is 17. Can you beat it? </p>

## Controls 🚗
<p> WASD or ARROW KEYS for movement </p>
<p> Once you reach the ending screen, press spacebar to replay the game again. The score should be updated only if you get a faster time. </p>

## Future Plans 🚓
<p> This game will be remade in the future with more features! Keep a lookout for that!</p>
